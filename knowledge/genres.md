# Genre notes

Each section is one retrievable document. `related` lists catalog genres that a
fan of this genre is likely to enjoy; the recommender gives those partial credit.

## pop
type: genre
related: indie pop, synthwave, edm, latin
keywords: catchy mainstream radio chart hooks upbeat sing-along
Pop is built around short, catchy songs with strong hooks and bright production.

## indie pop
type: genre
related: pop, synthwave, folk
keywords: indie alternative bedroom jangly dreamy quirky
Indie pop keeps pop melodies but with a lighter, more handmade, alternative feel.

## k-pop
type: genre
related: pop, indie pop, edm, hip hop
keywords: korean kpop idol group choreography dance polished bright
K-pop is Korean pop music known for polished production, dance routines, and mixing pop, EDM, and hip hop.

## j-pop
type: genre
related: pop, indie pop, synthwave
keywords: japanese jpop anime city pop bright
J-pop is Japanese pop music, often bright and melodic, with ties to anime soundtracks and city pop.

## edm
type: genre
related: pop, synthwave, latin
keywords: electronic dance club festival drop rave house techno
EDM is electronic dance music made for clubs and festivals, with big builds and drops.

## house
type: genre
related: edm, pop, latin
keywords: house club four-on-the-floor dance electronic
House is a steady four-on-the-floor electronic dance style.

## synthwave
type: genre
related: edm, pop, indie pop
keywords: retro 80s synth neon night driving outrun
Synthwave uses 1980s-style synthesizers and drum machines for a retro, nighttime feel.

## rock
type: genre
related: metal, blues, indie pop
keywords: guitar band drums riffs loud live alternative
Rock is guitar-driven band music, often loud and energetic.

## punk
type: genre
related: rock, metal
keywords: punk fast loud short raw rebellious
Punk is fast, raw, short rock music.

## metal
type: genre
related: rock
keywords: heavy distortion screaming headbang loud riffs
Metal is heavy, distorted, and intense rock music.

## hip hop
type: genre
related: r&b, latin, pop
keywords: rap beats rhymes bars trap flow
Hip hop pairs rapped vocals with beats; it often feels confident and rhythmic.

## rap
type: genre
related: hip hop, r&b
keywords: rap rapper bars verses trap
Rap is the vocal style at the core of hip hop.

## r&b
type: genre
related: hip hop, jazz, pop
keywords: rnb soul smooth vocals groove slow jam
R&B is smooth, soulful vocal music with a groove.

## soul
type: genre
related: r&b, jazz, blues
keywords: soul gospel motown warm vocals
Soul is warm, gospel-rooted vocal music close to R&B and blues.

## jazz
type: genre
related: blues, r&b, lofi
keywords: saxophone piano swing improvisation cafe smooth
Jazz features improvisation, swing rhythms, and acoustic instruments like piano and sax.

## blues
type: genre
related: jazz, rock, country
keywords: blues guitar soulful sad twelve-bar
Blues is soulful, often sad guitar music that shaped jazz and rock.

## lofi
type: genre
related: jazz, ambient, hip hop
keywords: lo-fi lofi beats study chill relaxed hip hop instrumental
Lofi hip hop is relaxed, low-fidelity instrumental beat music, popular for studying.

## ambient
type: genre
related: lofi, classical
keywords: atmospheric drone soundscape calm meditation sleep
Ambient music is atmospheric and slow, meant to set a calm mood rather than demand attention.

## classical
type: genre
related: ambient, jazz
keywords: orchestra piano symphony sonata composer instrumental
Classical music is composed instrumental music for orchestra, piano, and other ensembles.

## country
type: genre
related: folk, blues
keywords: country twang banjo storytelling roads nashville
Country is storytelling music with guitars, fiddles, and roots in rural America.

## folk
type: genre
related: country, indie pop, classical
keywords: acoustic singer-songwriter campfire guitar storytelling
Folk is acoustic, storytelling music, often just voice and guitar.

## reggae
type: genre
related: latin, hip hop
keywords: jamaica offbeat island laid-back dub
Reggae is laid-back Jamaican music with an offbeat rhythm.

## latin
type: genre
related: reggae, pop, edm
keywords: reggaeton salsa spanish dance latino fiesta bachata
Latin covers dance styles like reggaeton and salsa, usually upbeat and playful.

## reggaeton
type: genre
related: latin, hip hop, pop
keywords: reggaeton dembow perreo spanish dance
Reggaeton is a Latin dance style with a dembow beat.
